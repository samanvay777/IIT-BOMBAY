01 ABOUT TECHFEST

NOT JUST A FESTIVAL.

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of IIT Bombay, bringing together students, innovators, researchers and enthusiasts from across the world.

From robotics and artificial intelligence to competitions, workshops, exhibitions, lectures and large-scale experiences, Techfest turns the IIT Bombay campus into a playground for ideas.
